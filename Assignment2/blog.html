From First Lines of Code to Future Technologies

Written by Daya | October 25, 2026

Every Big Idea Starts Somewhere

What if the technologies we imagine today become the inventions that shape tomorrow? That question is one of the reasons I find computer science so fascinating. From intelligent machines to futuristic flying technologies, there is so much left to explore.

My journey into this world is just beginning. As a first-year Computer Science Engineering student specialising in AI and Machine Learning, I am learning how even a few lines of code can turn an idea into something real.

Currently Loading: My Coding Journey

Right now, I am exploring Java through Data Structures and Algorithms and learning HTML through web development. Sometimes a program works perfectly; sometimes one tiny mistake sends me back to the code. Both experiences teach me something new.

I am also getting familiar with tools like VS Code, Git, and IntelliJ IDEA. They are becoming part of my everyday learning as I practise writing code, understanding errors, and building small projects.

Beyond the Screen

My curiosity does not stop at websites and programs. Artificial intelligence, robotics, and flying exosuits make me wonder how far human imagination and engineering can go.

I know that turning futuristic ideas into reality takes time, knowledge, experimentation, and plenty of problem-solving. That is what makes the journey exciting: there is always something new to learn.

A Note to My Future Self

One day, today's beginner projects may become the foundation for something I have only imagined so far. For now, keep learning, keep experimenting, and stay curious.
